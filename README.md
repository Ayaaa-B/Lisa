## Website Introduction 

Nails by Lisa is a home-based nail studio in East London, South Africa, founded in 2020.
The business offers high-quality acrylic sets, custom nail art, and pedicures with a focus on hygiene, 
durability, and detailed craftsmanship. To streamline operations and improve client service, 
the business is launching a website www.nailsbylisa.co.za.

## The website Index & Core Structure
The upcoming website is designed with a responsive, mobile-first layout using soft minimalist tones 
like Rose Gold, Dark Emerald Green, and Deep Black. 
The site architecture is organized into five main sections which are the following: 

### Homepage: Displays core brand highlights, key studio announcements, high-resolution visual showcases, and quick booking access.
---
### About Us: The site will highlight Lisa's background include her core business values, and commitment to hygiene and precision.
---
### Services & Pricing: The site outlines itemized pricing for acrylic overlays, tip sets, toe reconstructions, and design add-ons.
---
### Studio Policies & FAQ: Clearly details deposit rules as well as cancellation windows, guest limits, and the 3-day feedback policy.
---
### Booking & Contact Portal: Integrates the step-by-step submission form, Absa payment details, proof-of-payment upload options, and direct Instagram links.

1. Organisation Overview
Nails by Lisa was established in 2020, Nails by Lisa is a successful home-based nail studio that’s located in East London. Built on a passion for nail care, the studio has grown a dedicated client base by prioritizing quality over speed, delivering long-lasting sets and custom artistry to its clients. To provide meticulously craft high-quality acrylic and pedicure services in a comfortable setting to ensuring maximum retention as well as a unique custom art, and complete client satisfaction.
The end goal for this business is to become East London’s premier boutique home studio brand that is known for artistic excellence, seamless online client experiences, and standard setting hygiene and booking policies. The target audience for this business are women and individuals residing in or around East London who will appreciate detailed nail artistry and long-lasting acrylic sets that are professional, this also includes pedicures and premium personal care, while being active on social media platforms like Instagram.
 
2. Website Goals and Objectives
Define Specific Goals for the Website:
Bookings: Send a Instagram direct message to request booking an appointment and then pay a R50 deposit to secure your spot.
Centralize Information: Provide clients clear access to the service menu, add-on pricing, banking details, and strict studio policies to avoid repetitive client inquiries about the details of the appointment.
Showcase Portfolio: Highlight the high-resolution galleries of acrylic overlays including custom designs and long length options that way when people visit the site, they know what kind of work they will be paying for
Build Brand Professionalism: Elevate the brand presence beyond social media with a dedicated digital store interface this will make it easy on both lisa and the clients.
Key Performance Indicators:
•	Conversion Rate: Percentage of website visitors who complete a booking inquiry or deposit transaction this way lisa is able to work with her calendar and future customer can see on the site what days are available.
•	Reduced Inquiry Volume: Decrease in manual DMs asking basic questions like pricing, house call inquiries and banking info.
•	Website Traffic: Monthly active visitors and page views this way when the site is viewed lisa can know which nail sets are the most popular and which one most client dislike making for great recommendations to clients.
•	Bounce Rate: Target under 40%, ensuring customers can find lisa’s business policies and menu information easily.

•	Implement an automated web interface that presents studio policies clearly before deposit confirmation so that clients know what the requirements of booking lisa are.
•	Create a structured gallery and price calculator for services and add-ons like French tips, rhinestones and design changes to keep herself and clients informed.
•	Streamline payment confirmation guidance for immediate EFT transfers via Absa to make it easier for her clients and new customers.
