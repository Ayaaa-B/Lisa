Our History

Founded in 2020, Nails by Lisa started as a passionate boutique nail studio in East London, South Africa. Over the years, we have grown into a trusted home studio dedicated to precision acrylic application, custom nail artistry, and long-lasting quality sets.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide an unhurried, hygienic, and luxurious nail care experience. We strive to maintain natural nail health while crafting durable, beautiful designs that leave every client feeling confident.

Our Vision

Our vision is to remain East London's premier boutique home studio, setting high standards in nail artistry, detailed techniques, and exceptional client satisfaction.
