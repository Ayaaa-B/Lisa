OUR SERVICES & PRICING

At Nails by Lisa, we specialize in high-quality acrylic enhancements and pedicures with an emphasis on maximum retention, precision, and unhurried luxury craftsmanship.

Acrylic & Pedicure Services

  • Acrylic Overlay: Natural nail reinforcement using premium acrylic.
  • Full Set with Tips: Custom-length extensions with tailored shaping.
  • Pedicure (Plain Overlay): Clean toe prep paired with a smooth acrylic overlay.
  • Pedicure (Toe Reconstruction): Detailed restoration and reshaping for damaged or uneven toenails.

Custom Art & Add-Ons

  • French Tips: Classic or colored hand-painted French design.
  • Rhinestones & Gems: Premium placement per accent nail or full set.
  • Glitter Polish & Encapsulation: Sparkle overlay or embedded artwork.
  • Mid-Service Design Changes: Custom adjustments available during your session.
